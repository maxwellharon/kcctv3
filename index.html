Who we serve

Every woman aged 20 and above is welcome.

We focus on women aged 20 and above, with no upper age limit — screening protects at every age. Come as you are, whatever your work or your story. The same test, the same care, the same respect for every woman.

  • No judgement and no questions about your life. You are welcome here exactly as you are.
  • Girls aged 9–14 can be vaccinated against HPV, when the vaccine works best.
  • All women of reproductive age are encouraged to be vaccinated as well.

We value confidentiality

Your name, your results and your story stay with the people who need them to care for you — and with nobody else. We never ask a woman to explain what she does for a living.

We are politically neutral

We work with anyone who shares our aim of protecting women’s health. The Trust does not support any political party or candidate.

Good to know

Questions women ask us.

Is screening free?

Yes. Our mission is to provide free HPV screening, and colposcopy is free too when it is needed.

Will anyone find out I came?

No. We value confidentiality. Your details and results are shared only with the people involved in your care, and only for that purpose — never with an employer, family or anyone else unless you say so.

Who can be screened?

Women aged 20 and above, with no upper age limit. Girls aged 9–14 can be vaccinated against HPV, which is when the vaccine works best, and all women of reproductive age are encouraged to be vaccinated too.

How soon do I get my result?

The rapid HPV E6/E7 Oncoprotein test is done at the point of care, so you get your result the same day.

Know a woman who hasn’t been screened? Tell her.

One conversation can lead to one test, and one test can save a life. Call or WhatsApp us — privately.
