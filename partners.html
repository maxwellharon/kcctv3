Become a partner

Hospitals, laboratories, organisations and companies are welcome. Let’s talk.

We value secrecy

Every partner works to our confidentiality standard. A woman’s details and results are shared only with the people directly involved in her care, and never beyond that without her permission.

Politically neutral

We partner on the basis of care, not politics. The Trust supports no political party or candidate, and we ask the same of the work we do together.

Work with us

Ways to partner.

We shape each partnership around what you can offer and what women need most.

Medical partner

If you run a hospital, clinic or laboratory, you can offer screening sites, treatment, colposcopy or diagnostics for the women we reach.

Community organisation

Already working with women in your area, or with peer groups and outreach teams? We share training, screening and referral routes; you bring trust.

Corporate or foundation

Fund a camp, a day of screening or a vaccination outreach, and see exactly what it made possible.

Individual supporter

Give once or monthly, volunteer your skills, or simply tell a woman you know about free screening.
