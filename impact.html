What the numbers mean

Every figure is a woman.

Behind each number is a woman who was reached close to home, tested with a rapid HPV E6/E7 Oncoprotein test, and either reassured or treated — often on the same day.

Girls and women vaccinated against HPV are protected from the virus that causes almost all cervical cancer. Women found to need treatment were treated, or linked to further investigation.
